# Project Proposal: Shopno Superstore Promotional Sales Analysis

## 1. Research Question
How do different promotional activities impact the daily aggregate sales volume at Shopno Superstore, and can we predict future sales bumps based on planned promotional spend?

## 2. Dataset
**Source:** [Kaggle: Supermarket Sales Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aungpyaeap/supermarket-sales)
**Description:** Historical records of supermarket sales across multiple branches. We will engineer features to simulate specific promotional days to run our **Regression** models.

## 3. Existing Work (Two-Source Scan)
1. **"The Impact of Sales Promotions on Retail Profitability" (Smith et al., 2019):** Demonstrates that temporary price reductions increase short-term sales volume. 
2. **"Predictive Modeling of Retail Sales Forecasting" (Chen & Wang, 2021):** Utilized Multiple Linear Regression on retail data, finding that feature-engineered promotional flags improved models significantly.
